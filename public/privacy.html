Privacy

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. GitHub Pages serves it, and like any web server it sees your IP address and browser when you open the page.

The real models

Each scene has a folded panel, “Try a real model on one photo”. Nothing in it runs or downloads until you press Run the model. Your photo stays on your device: the model runs in your browser, and the photo is never uploaded.

  • Reading a label (Tesseract): every file comes from this site. No other company is contacted.
  • Finding parts and materials (OWL-ViT): the model’s files, about 150 MB, come from Hugging Face, Inc., a company in the United States, through its Amazon CloudFront servers. Hugging Face sees your IP address and browser, as any website does. This site tells your browser not to send it the address of this page.

The reason is the one you gave by pressing Run: the model can only run in your browser if your browser downloads it. Hugging Face handles those requests under its own privacy policy, at huggingface.co/privacy.

What stays in your browser

Your browser keeps the downloaded model files, so the next run is fast. They stay until you press Remove downloaded models in the panel, or clear this site’s data in your browser. Nothing is sent back to this site or anyone else.

Who is responsible, and your rights

Simon van Lierde runs this site. Under the GDPR you can ask what data about you is processed, and have it corrected or deleted. This site itself stores none; for the model downloads, Hugging Face is the one to ask. To reach me, open an issue at the site’s GitHub repository. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the Netherlands, that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
