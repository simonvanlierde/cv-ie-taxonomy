# Roadmap

Last reviewed: 2026-11-07. Planning horizon: through 2027-11-07.

This site helps readers understand the evidence in the review article and try
small examples in their browser. The [README](../README.md) describes its current
features. These plans describe the scope of maintenance, not delivery guarantees.

## Planned

- Review the closing narrative and photo guidance with readers. Keep consent,
  privacy and the limits of the evidence clear.
- Preserve an identifiable version for the article's submission and keep the
  site, references and article links consistent with the research record.
- Run reader sessions and repair the usability or accessibility problems they
  identify. Keep a plain-text route to the ratings.
- Maintain the build, dependencies, security checks and browser tests. Correct
  defects and update the documentation when behaviour changes.

## Not planned

- Change the paper's ratings based on a visitor's photo or assessment edits.
- Add accounts or a server-side photo collection system to this static companion.
- Turn the illustrative model panels into a validated repair, recycling or
  material-identification service.
- Add a general model benchmark or promise support for every browser and device.
